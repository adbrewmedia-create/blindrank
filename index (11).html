How it works

Each round deals ten Premier League players, one at a time. Place each one in a slot from 1 (highest potential) to 10 before you see the next. A placed player is locked. When all ten are down, the true order is revealed.

Scoring

Every slot you are away from a player's true position costs 2 points, so a perfect order scores 100. Exact hits show green, one slot out shows yellow, and anything further shows red.
